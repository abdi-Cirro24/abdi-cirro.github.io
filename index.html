Introduction

About me

A brief overview of my academic background and professional interests.

Welcome to my academic and professional website. I am Abdi Ibrahim, a lecturer at Jigjiga University. I hold an undergraduate degree in Public Administration and Development Management from Dire Dawa University and a Master's degree in Educational Leadership and Management (EDLM) from Haramaya University.

My professional interests include education systems, education management information systems (EMIS), educational leadership, instructional supervision, inclusive education, development management and community engagement.

This website provides an overview of my education, professional interests, research areas and academic work.
